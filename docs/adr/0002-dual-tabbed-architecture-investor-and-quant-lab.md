# Dual-Tabbed Architecture: Investor Growth & Quant Lab

We structured QuantNiti with four primary tabs: `Grow` (AI Baskets & Growth Projections), `Explore` (Individual Stock Profiles), `Quant Lab` (Regime & Technical Strategy Backtesting), and `Portfolio` (Virtual Investment Tracker). This resolves the tension between non-finance retail users (and the academic evaluation panel) who need simple, jargon-free portfolio growth recommendations, and quantitative traders/researchers who require in-depth technical backtesting and regime analysis.
