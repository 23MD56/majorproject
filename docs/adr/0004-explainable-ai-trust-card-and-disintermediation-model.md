# Explainable AI Trust Card and Disintermediation Moat

To directly answer "Why trust this over a bank wealth manager?", every recommendation is paired with a four-pillar Trust Card detailing: (1) Current market regime context, (2) Historical backtested hit rate, (3) Maximum stress drawdown guardrail, and (4) Disintermediation cost savings (0% commission vs 1.5-2.5% wealth management fees).
