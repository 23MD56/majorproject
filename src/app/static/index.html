Why Not Groww or INDmoney?

Groww & INDmoney excel at tracking past performance (Active & Lifetime XIRR). However, they are 100% backward-looking, lack forward probabilistic projections, and offer zero automated regime-adaptive crash protection. QuantNiti empowers retail investors with forward predictive allocation.

Why Not Zerodha Streak or TradingView?

Streak and TradingView operate on single tickers in isolation with technical rules or Pine Script. They cannot construct, optimize, or backtest multi-stock portfolios. QuantNiti performs portfolio-level Hierarchical Risk Parity across the NIFTY 50 with zero coding required.

Why Not Smallcase?

Smallcase charges recurring manager subscription fees (₹2,000–₹10,000/yr) that erode retail capital compounding, and rebalances on rigid calendar schedules. QuantNiti offers 0% middleman fees and dynamically alerts on market regime shifts.
